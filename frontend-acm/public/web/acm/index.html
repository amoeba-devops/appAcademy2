ACM — Academy Management

Operated by Amoeba Company (아메바컴퍼니) · support@amoeba.site

ACM is a multi-tenant web service that helps private academies (hagwon) run their daily operations: new-student consultations, student and parent records, teachers and class schedules, tuition payments, notices, and internal projects, issues and documents. Each academy (tenant) uses ACM through its own administrator account.

ACM은 학원 운영을 위한 멀티 테넌트 웹 서비스입니다. 신규 상담, 학생·학부모 관리, 강사·수업 일정, 수납, 공지, 그리고 업무용 프로젝트·이슈·문서를 하나의 화면에서 관리합니다. 각 학원(테넌트)은 자체 관리자 계정으로 ACM을 사용합니다.

How ACM uses Google · Google 연동

An academy's service operator may connect the academy's company Google Workspace account so that files uploaded or created in ACM projects are saved in a shared folder in that account's Google Drive. Personal Gmail accounts are not accepted.

  • Scopes requested: openid, email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.
  • openid / email — to identify which company account is connected and to show its address and domain to the academy's administrators.
  • drive.file — to create the academy's shared folder, create Google Docs/Sheets, upload files into that folder, and open files the user explicitly picks with Google Picker. ACM cannot see other files in the account's Drive.
  • ACM does not read Gmail, contacts or calendars, does not change Google sharing settings, and does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

학원의 서비스 운영자는 학원의 회사 Google Workspace 계정을 연결할 수 있습니다. 연결하면 ACM 프로젝트에서 업로드·작성한 파일이 해당 계정 Google Drive의 공용폴더에 저장됩니다. 일반 Gmail 계정은 연결할 수 없습니다. 요청 권한은 계정 확인(openid, email)과 ACM이 만들거나 사용자가 직접 선택한 파일에 한정된 Drive 권한(drive.file)뿐입니다. 자세한 내용은 개인정보처리방침을 참고하세요.
